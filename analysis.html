3. Explaining the differences

(i) Self-reported auxiliary data

We know that the proprietary algorithm developed by Select Research used self-reported data on the app for Sex, Age, Height and Weight to project the 3D avatar of respondents and estimate body volume biomarkers. Could the differences in waist circumference we observe between the app and interviewer-aided self-measurement be driven by a bias in the information respondents provide about themselves in the app on these 4 dimensions?

Age

Only 5/98 observations (5%) revealed differences in self-reported age in the app compared to the survey. Only 2% were significant differences in age (5 and 11 years).

Sex

Only 1 case of difference in reported sex between the survey and the app.

Height

47/98 (48%) of reported heights are different in the app v survey, 12 (25%) of these were reported downwards in the app. In only 22 cases were the reported discrepancies bigger than 1cm.

The plot below highlights the respondents for whom there was a difference in reported height in the app (∆Height). The darker the dot, the larger was the height difference in cm.

Weight

52/98 (53%) of reported heights are different in the app v survey, 22 (42%) of these were reported downwards in the app. In only 23 cases were the reported discrepancies bigger than 1kg.

The plot below highlights the respondents for whom there was a difference in reported weight in the app (∆Height). The darker the dot, the larger was the height difference in kg.
