Compliance

Table 1. Respondent Compliance in waist+hip measurement modules
Self-Measurement Self-Measurement w/ Interviewer BVI App
Compliance Rate [Waist] 56.2% (n=1683) 95.5% (n=22) 69.8% (n=497)
Compliance Rate [Hip] 54.2% (n=1683) 95.5% (n=22) 24.7% (n=497)

 

With respect to the BVI app, the table shows us the rate of compliance in relation to those respondents who installed and logged in the app (n=497). We can also compute a separate rate of compliance: out of 1565 respondents who were asked to use the BVI app, only 497 reported to have installed and logged in successfully (compliance rate of 32%).

However, from these 497, we only received 347 measurements of waist circumference and 123 measurements of hip circumference, hence the compliance rates shown in Table 1. It’s unclear whether the missing measurements for waist (n=150) and for hip (n=224) are cause by non-compliance from the respondent (even after installing and logging on) or whether they were excluded because the measurements provided was faulty/undefined.
